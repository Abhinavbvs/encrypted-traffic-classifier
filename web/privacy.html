MetaFlow/Privacy
Legal

Nothing about you is collected.

This website

This site sets no cookies, loads no analytics and sends no forms anywhere. The contact form is a local preview. One value in your browser's local storage remembers that you closed the privacy notice. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

The classifier

MetaFlow is built so that privacy does not depend on policy. It reads packet headers to build flows, discards payload bytes as they are parsed, and computes timing statistics only. No content, credentials or personal data reach the model.

When the optional cloud explanation tier is enabled, only the predicted class, its probabilities and the timing features are sent to the provider you configure. Leave the key unset to keep everything local.
