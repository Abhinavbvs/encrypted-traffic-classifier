Five parts. One pipeline.

MetaFlow is a chain of small, testable pieces, from raw capture to an explained verdict. Each one can be used on its own.

01

PCAP ingestion

Native Scapy parser builds 5-tuple flows and throws payload bytes away on arrival.

→02

Timing features

Inter-arrival times, throughput, active bursts and idle gaps, computed per flow.

→03

LightGBM classifier

Selected over Random Forest and XGBoost by 5-fold stratified cross-validation.

→04

Explainable AI

Local Ollama, cloud LLM, or an offline rule engine, routed automatically.

→05

Unknown detection

An adjustable confidence threshold turns novel traffic into a triage queue.
